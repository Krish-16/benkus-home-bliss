About Us

Hi, I’m the maker behind Benku’s Home Bliss Studio! Born out of a passion for handmade craftsmanship and Indian textile artistry, my studio creates unique designer cushions that bring warmth and elegance to every corner of your home — be it your sofa, bed, zula, or gifting moments.

I believe that cushions are more than just decor — they’re expressions of comfort, creativity, and personality.

From my sewing table to your living room—I hope every cushion adds a little bliss to your home.

– Khushbu
